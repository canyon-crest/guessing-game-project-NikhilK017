Guessing Game

Welcome to the guessing game! Try to guess the right number (randomly generated) in the fewest amount of guesses! There are three levels to choose from. After each guess, you will know if your guess was hot, warm, or cold. Good luck!

You can hover over the difficulty levels to see how they're different.

You can hit Enter to submit your guess, and every time you hit you re-guess, the input box will be selected for you (so you can guess quicker).

For help understanding how the ratings and outputs work (or to cheat and guess faster), you can select Show answer...
